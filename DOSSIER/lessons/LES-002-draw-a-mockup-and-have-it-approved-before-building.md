## Statement

For anything a person will look at, draw a mockup with invented data and have the reader approve it before writing requirements or code. A page specified in words met every requirement and was still rejected; the page built from an approved mockup was accepted at once.

## Valid for

Pages, reports and other output made for people; not command output made for agents.
