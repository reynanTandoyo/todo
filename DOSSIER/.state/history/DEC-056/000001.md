## Decision

Every entry can carry a summary: one or two plain sentences about it, written for people, shown when an entry is opened on the page and in Last 7 days. Titles stay as agents write them. The planner writes the summary, writes the done note as the result in one sentence, and gives a reason a person understands when dropping or giving back a task. (Owner, 2026-11-21)

## Reason

Titles and accept lines are written by agents for agents; the page is only as readable as the texts behind it.

## Rejected

Enforcing a title length on the planner.
