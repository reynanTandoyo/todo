## Decision

Orca orchestration workers and subagents run on Claude Haiku with xhigh effort. A stronger model is used only when the owner names it for one specific task; the exception does not carry over to the next. So far the exceptions were the requirements tasks T-086 (v0.6 and v0.7) and T-146 (v0.8), which ran on Opus.

## Reason

The owner wants cheap workers for everything and decides each exception. Writing the requirements is most of the thinking, so that is where a strong model was allowed.

## Rejected

Sonnet, Opus or Fable for ordinary worker tasks.
