## Decision

Every entry has one kind, taken from its type: task, milestone, question or idea. A question may name the entry it is about (ask --about). An idea is recorded with todo idea, is never handed out as work, and becomes a task only with promote. The kinds are separate lists in the views, not separate files; todo list keeps its default and gains --kind. (Owner, 2026-11-18)

## Reason

Ideas and questions must be kept without being counted as open work or handed to an agent; one file is what the lock and the merge driver protect.

## Rejected

Separate files per kind.
