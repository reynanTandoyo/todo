## Decision

The page of v0.7 is replaced by the page approved in docs/report-mockup.html. In reading order: one sentence and one bar for the whole project; Needs you; Last 7 days; All work as one tree in which done and cancelled entries stay under their milestone; one line per entry, with a second line only for in progress, waiting and cancelled; details on a click. The five states are named in plain words: to do, in progress, waiting, done, cancelled. Cancelled tasks are not counted in the total. Planner internals move to a closed part For the planner. The mockup is drawn and approved before any code is written. (Owner, 2026-11-21)

## Reason

The owner and a colleague must know within a minute what is done, what was cancelled and why, what is still to do and what needs them. The v0.7 page, specified in words only, had too much text, too little context and a confusing tree.

## Rejected

A modern website with animation, themes or sound; specifying the page in words only.
