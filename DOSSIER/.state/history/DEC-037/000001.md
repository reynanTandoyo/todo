## Decision

Settings are optional: one todo.toml next to TODO.md, no per-user settings. The one per-user file is the project register, which lists the user's projects and holds no settings. (Amended by the owner's decision of 2026-11-18)

## Reason

The register is per user because it spans that user's projects in different folders, and it lets todo show all of them without searching the PC.
