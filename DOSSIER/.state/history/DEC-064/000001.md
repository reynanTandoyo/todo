## Decision

From 2026-11-26 this project has a dossier (code TODO, a code project). Tasks stay in TODO.md and are managed by todo; decisions, assumptions, findings, lessons, key values, the glossary and the code map are in the DOSSIER folder and are managed by dossier. Because dossier was introduced late, the entries up to v1.0 were filled in afterwards from docs/requirements.md, docs/research.md, the task archive and the sessions; their dates are the day they were recorded, and the day each was decided is named in its text. (Owner, 2026-11-26)

## Reason
