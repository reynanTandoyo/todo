## Decision

A task has one of five statuses: open, claimed, blocked, done, dropped. Changes follow one transition table; anything else is refused with BAD_TRANSITION and the commands allowed from the current status.

## Reason

dropped keeps "will not do" out of the done count and out of the way. Earlier scripts had no unblock and no dropped; one file had 25 blocked tasks.

## Rejected

Seven statuses as in another tool studied: more than agents need.
