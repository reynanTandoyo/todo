## Decision

Every agent learns the tool from a short common rules block of at most 15 lines (todo help agents, AGENTS.md) plus one text per role: todo help worker, at most 10 lines, and todo help planner. A test fails when a text grows past its limit. A worker needs only todo next --claim --brief; the work order repeats the finishing commands. Hooks and a skill stay extras, and there is still no MCP server. (Amended by the owner's decision of 2026-11-19)

## Reason

Commands cost no context, but the rules text does: it grew from 11 to 20 lines by v0.7, and a weak model follows long rules badly. No agent should carry rules it does not need.

## Rejected

One rules text for every role.
