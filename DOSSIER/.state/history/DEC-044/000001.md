## Decision

Every task has a level (the difficulty field) from 1 to 5, given by the planner when the task is added; a task without one cannot exist and is never handed out. A level says how much of the work is still open: at level 1 everything is written down, so even the weakest agent finishes it without a mistake; level 5 is open and is split before anyone works on it. The intended way is to split until every task is level 1. As a last resort the planner may keep a task higher with set --final and a reason. No model name appears anywhere in todo; a project may describe its levels in todo.toml. Nothing is computed from the level, and todo never sets or changes one. (Owner, 2026-11-19)

## Reason

The owner wants to spawn cheap agents such as Haiku or DeepSeek flash for everything, because they cost almost nothing; that only works when each task leaves nothing to explore, guess or decide.

## Rejected

A computed score from priority and difficulty; model names in the tool; raising a level automatically after a failure.
