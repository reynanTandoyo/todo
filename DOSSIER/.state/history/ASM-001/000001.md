## Assumption

A task that names its files, numbered steps, a check, why and accept can be finished without a mistake by the weakest agents, such as Haiku or DeepSeek flash.

## Reason

The whole design of v0.8 rests on it, but it has not been measured: there is no count of level-1 tasks done, failed or given back per model. v1.1 is the first work planned completely in level-1 tasks.
