## Decision

The main agent decides design questions itself, says which decisions it made, and asks the owner only about very important things: what becomes public (every push), reversing an earlier decision of the owner, and choices only a person can make. (Owner, 2026-11-15)

## Reason

The owner wants one finished tool with minimal back-and-forth.

## Rejected

Asking the owner about every design detail.
