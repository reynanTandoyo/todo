## Assumption

The colour-blind colour sets (blue, orange, greys, stripes) keep the five states apart for a person with red-green colour blindness, in light and in dark mode.

## Reason

The colours were chosen from theory and a contrast test; the owner approved the page as a whole, but no test with each state side by side was recorded, and other kinds of colour blindness were not considered.
